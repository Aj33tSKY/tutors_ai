# Fresh visual review of the Kindling hero

Reviewed 2 November 2026. This is a design assessment, not a proposed product-source change. [Open the visual board](index.html) or [contact sheet](comparison.webp).

## Verdict

Kindling already has a distinctive product demonstration. The serif dialogue, two voices, coloured evidence and connected analysis explain a specific benefit better than an abstract AI orb. Keep those assets. The next gain comes from stronger composition, scale and light: make the conversation the dominant object and the insight its visual climax. Adding more independent effects would dilute the current strength.

At thumbnail scale, our headline and green CTA remain clear, but the product demonstration splits into three small targets: a tall conversation panel and two narrow notes. The photo has almost as much contrast as the useful foreground. The key breakthrough is therefore less immediately legible than the recognisable objects in the strongest references.

## What was actually inspected

Live desktop screenshots of Kindling plus seven reference sites, all at 1440 × 1000px. Optional cookie overlays were rejected for cleaner captures where possible. Raycast and Resend were recaptured with software WebGL because the first GPU-disabled captures omitted their hero artwork; the final saved images include it. These are current captures, not remembered historical designs.

Kindling's capture uses reduced motion to show both completed turns and insights; the references use normal motion. Therefore the board supports composition/material/typography comparisons, not claims of matched animation performance. Motion choreography below is a proposed adaptation, not a claim that another site uses exactly that sequence. No mobile competitor review or conversion testing was performed. Reference screenshots are research artifacts, not assets to ship on the public page.

## Keep from ours

- Real face crops, plain bold participant labels and opposing transcript direction make the two people clear.
- Newsreader for spoken words against Geist for analysis is an effective human/intelligence distinction.
- Whole-word transcript reveal, coloured evidence and curved annotation threads are a useful visual story, not empty decoration.
- The green headline and CTA give a strong focal point and recognisable brand colour.
- The photograph adds humanity that the darker reference sites often lack.

## Where ours looks weaker

1. The tall main card contains a large vertical pause between voices, while the meaningful AI notes are only about 207px wide. The scale hierarchy does not yet match the value hierarchy.
2. The dark laptop corner, writing hand and high-contrast notebook lines compete with the delicate ribbons and analysis. Frosted cards help readability but do not fix the composition outside them.
3. The main card crosses the white/photo seam by about 70px. That can be an intentional editorial overlap, but currently the seam, card edge and headline column do not form an obvious alignment system.
4. Fine waveform strands can disappear at reduced scale. Bigger amplitude alone would make them busy; a controlled brighter core and subtle material layering would improve presence more effectively.
5. The analysis is valuable but reads like small helper text. In the references, the important hero object is recognisable at a glance. Here the payoff requires leaning in to read.

## Six borrowable changes, in order

| 3 | **Recompose the demo as one authored object.** Reduce unused vertical space, enlarge the ribbon region slightly, and deliberately align card top, notes and baseline to a consistent grid. Either make the seam crossing clearly intentional with a stronger overlap, or align the whole stage within the photo. | 4 | **Add restrained material depth to the ribbons.** Keep the measured organic motion. ## Strongest coherent direction

**An editorial tutoring scene with a luminous conversation.** Combine Granola's authored layering, Mercury's photographic negative space and ElevenLabs/Resend's controlled material quality. Keep the existing human photographs, serif dialogue, organic ribbons and annotation threads. Prioritise ranks 1–3 together; then refine ribbon material and timing. This improves the entire scene rather than increasing the effect count.

Do not pivot to a generic black AI hero, more gradients, floating particles, 3D tilted cards, a cursor-following glow or a logo cloud without genuine customers. Those could look flashy but would weaken the human teaching story that already differentiates Kindling.

## Suggested next comparison

Make two static visual variants before modifying animation logic: A) existing structure with a quieter photo crop and larger notes; B) a more compact, deliberately aligned demo with the same changes. Capture both beside the current hero at 1440px and 390px. Choose the strongest silhouette first, then add the motion polish. That makes the decision visual and easy to reverse.
