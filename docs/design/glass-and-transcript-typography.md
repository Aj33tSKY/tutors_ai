# Glass and transcript typography

The hero uses more transparent surfaces (60% conversation, 64% insights) with 44px backdrop blur. Only the view through the cards is blurred; the surrounding photograph stays sharp. Fine inset highlights define the glass edges without a heavy border.

Spoken transcripts use self-hosted Newsreader, while profiles, controls and AI analysis retain Geist. Newsreader also appears in the lesson-memory quotations to carry the same human voice through the page. Its OFL license is stored beside the font. Insight copy is slightly larger, and hovering an insight gently strengthens its source highlight without moving either element or the connector.

## References

- [Production Type: Newsreader](https://productiontype.com/font/newsreader) — an on-screen reading family with an editorial character.
- [Granola: A new look](https://www.granola.ai/blog/a-new-look-for-granola) — combines expressive typography with a clear interface face and a restrained visual system.

## Further opportunities

A small scenario selector could let visitors explore a hint, a misconception and a breakthrough. Keep the current automatic rotation as the default.

A brief saved-insight transition could connect a highlighted phrase to the lesson-memory section, making the product's lasting value tangible. Respect reduced motion and avoid adding another permanently animated element.

Avoid adding more badges, decorative gradients or competing typefaces: the photograph, waveform and spoken typography already provide the main contrast.
